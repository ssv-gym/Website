    About SSV

    Shree Siddhi Vinayak Gym

    SSV Gym is a Virar West gym for strength training, CrossFit, cardio and functional fitness, whether you are just starting out or training for a goal.

    Train with a personal trainer, follow a weight loss or weight gain programme, and recover in the steam room after your session.

    • Weight loss and weight gain programmes
    • CrossFit and functional training
    • Complimentary lockers
